# Voodoo skin — opt-in (STANDARDS §9)

Neon on near-black, per `reference/art/ART_STYLE.md`, with the voodoo motifs:
pieces → skulls, crossbones and voodoo dolls (`scripts/common/voodoo.gd`,
drawn in code), card backs → the voodoo doll, accents → the owner's **Lips**
motif (`media/common/art/lips.*`, not delivered yet).

- Turned on per game in ⚙ Options → Look, or for every game by the app-wide
  "💀 Skull mode" (it flips the default; a game's own pick wins).
- Each player's skin is local: online opponents can see different pieces.
- Games with a Voodoo skin today: Four in a Row (red skulls vs yellow dolls),
  Tic-Tac-Toe (crossbones vs skulls), Reversi, Alien Attack, Gem Match,
  Bop the Mole (skull pieces), Checkers (neon board), Solitaire (neon cards,
  doll backs). Every other game's current neon look is its only look until
  it gets a Classic skin (Phase 5, one category at a time).
