# Neon Blast (id `geometry_wars`)

Shown as "Neon Blast" since pack v17 (voodoo re-theme for trademark distance; "Geometry Wars" is Activision's, never in store text). Horned voodoo skull firing pins at neon spirits; souls (were geoms), seals (gates), familiars (drones). **Code ids, file names, pack id and enemy ids keep old names** (`_core.gd` header maps them). Stat keys were renamed once with a migration (`HELP.RENAMED_STATS`, `_move_renamed_stats()` on open) — the one exception to "never rename save keys"; don't repeat (new label via a new key only for new stats).

Files (pack only): `geometry_wars_levels.gd` (rules for every mode as data: `CLASSIC` modes + 40 campaign `LEVELS`), `_core.gd` (enemies/collisions), `_bosses.gd`, `_drones.gd`, `_campaign.gd` (map + progress `user://geometry_wars_campaign.json`), `_sounds.gd`, `arena_canvas.gd`, `_game.gd`, `_ship.gd`.

## Skins
Classic = original neon-geometry look (darts, shapes, yellow geoms, `Core.CLASSIC_COLORS`, `arena_canvas.gd` `_classic` functions: keep as they were). Voodoo = pins, spirits, bright yellow/green souls. Both fly the owner's horned demon skull (`geometry_wars_ship.gd`, traced from their drawing; flies mouth first, red slits, flame that leaps on speed-up `flame`/`flame_boost`). Old claw ship gone (too close to Geometry Wars'; owner 2026-10-07). `_set_skin` sets `Core.classic` and the canvas. Bomb = see-through red bar down the right edge (bottom in portrait). Extra bombs/lives cost more each time (`EXTRA_GROWTH`), max `MAX_BOMBS`/`MAX_LIVES` 5.

## Modes + Campaign
- Modes: Endless (leaderboard), Time Attack, Unarmed, Sanctuary, Stampede, Coffin, Boss Rush (were Evolved/Deadline/Pacifism/King/Waves/Claustrophobia); Campaign + Cursed (were Adventure/Hardcore). Each has "Best score (<mode>)". **Only Endless and Campaign open at first**: campaign boss levels unlock modes (`Levels.MODE_UNLOCK`: 6 Time Attack, 12 Unarmed, 18 Stampede, 24 Sanctuary, 30 Coffin, 40 Boss Rush; `_home_modes()` builds the Landing; leaving the map reloads the scene).
- Campaign (reworked 2026-10-07): 6 worlds (6th Underworld opens after level 30). Levels 1-2 teach flying then bombs (`kills` goals, `hint` banner); from 3 every level is `survive`: clock out = cleared (banked) and play continues in **overtime**, busier each second (`OVERTIME_RAMP`), until last life or 🏁 Finish. **Stars are points**: ★ clearing, ★★/★★★ the level's `stars` targets (~0.6x and ~2.5x the bot's score at the clock; ★★★ needs overtime; levels 1, 2, 12, 30, 40 and Sanctuary ones by hand). Boss levels are survival too; beating the boss records `beaten` and opens prizes. Cursed = same levels, no familiar, starred apart. Extra bombs/lives come 1.5x as often (`EXTRA_EASE`).
- Campaign map: two star maps from owner's ChatGPT art (`games/geometry_wars/map_sun.jpg`, `map_blackhole.jpg`), every star a level; `Campaign.MAPS` holds [x, y, r] per level in picture pixels (bright-spot detection). 1-24 sun map (bosses on big flaring stars, sun = 24), 25-40 black-hole map (hole = 40). Tap a star for its card; ◀ ▶ switch maps.
- Bigger maps + camera: view 19 squares tall (`Core.U` fixed per game; positions in pixels); maps `MAP_SCALE`x bigger except tiny ones; grid simulates/draws only near the view. Bombs send a shockwave ring (`arena_canvas.shock()`).
- Black holes (`well`): sleeping pull gently; awake eat and grow (`Core.well_scale/well_range`), burst into protons. Burst plays a bubble pop, ducks effects, loops ~1 s (`sounds.duck`; music plays on), then "skitter" loop fades in while protons are loose.

## Bosses (`_bosses.gd` header)
Queen 6, serpent 12, scorpion 18, watcher 24, warden 30, titan 33, lord 35, watcher+warden 40. Boss Rush = queen, serpent, scorpion, titan, watcher, warden, lord at tiers 1-7 (`rush` entries [kind, tier]). Event `n` = **tier** 1-8: bigger (`size_k_of`), tougher (`hp_k_of`, +15%/tier), quicker (`rate_k`), more minions, extra attacks at set tiers. Bosses hold minions back while `BOSS_MINION_CAP` enemies are out. Saves from before the change dropped (`SAVE_VERSION`).
- Owner's Boss Rush feedback (2026-10-07): Hive Queen great as first (tier 1 kept, only bigger). Serpent's whole body shootable, slower, looser, hatches little bone serpents. Gravity Lord too hard (less health, pull, bending, fewer shots; moved to level 35). Titan "tough, looks great". **Bone Scorpion**: claws, then stinger (only while tail out after a strike), then head; before every strike it stops, rattles and marks the spot red for `WIND_UP` s. **Watcher**: eye whose beam and bolts stop at tombstones (`game.blocks`, Rect2s player can't cross, pins pass); lid shut = armoured, dazed after a beam = double damage; sight line locks `BEAM_LOCK` s before firing (blinking). **Warden**: shield fed by four corner pylons; down `SHIELD_DOWN` s then pylons regrow weaker.
- Balance (throwaway bot: dodges, chases souls, hides behind tombstones, runs to pylons): every level cleared except Sanctuary (bot can't use circles); from level 30 it needs a familiar (owner: "by the warden they should have a good drone"): with upgraded Raven Warden 3/3, Titan (tier 6) 2/3, Gravity Lord 3/3, Twin Terror 1/4 (hardest on purpose).

## Familiars (`_drones.gd` header)
Start with none; Raven joins when first boss beaten, the rest at campaign star totals (`Drones.UNLOCK_STARS`, all stars added). Every campaign score (won or lost) fills the flying familiar's pool (`fam.<kind>.xp`), which buys upgrades (deliberately expensive): armor (health 3 + 2/level; knocked out at 0), speed, power, soul pull (`UPGRADE_COST`, 5 levels each). Old saves' `points`/`owned` left unread. Follow on a loose spring with wander, own flame trail.

## Achievements for moves (`_help.gd`, `_record_moves()`)
Seals in one run, seal chains, close calls (`GRAZE_MARGIN`), chain reactions, biggest black hole broken, overtime, beams dodged behind a tombstone, Warden shields broken, flawless levels/boss kills.

## Sound + music
`_sounds.gd` (header = how-to): own voice pool (app Sfx has too few voices); every enemy has a spawn call; player death is a layered explosion; effects synthesized on a worker thread once, cached `user://geometry_wars_music/` (folder name kept; **bump `FX_VERSION` when a recipe changes**). Music from the Music library (docs/claude/music.md): adaptive director picks style by crowding (`set_intensity`: quick up, slow down), calm on death (`reset_calm`), next track every ~100 s, each switch a guarded `Music.play(tier, game, -1, fade)`; game over / campaign map = `Music.stop()`. The per-game 🎵 picker is gone (app-wide switch). Players' phones still hold old `<tier>_<n>_v1.pcm` (up to ~9 MB) and `user://geometry_wars_prefs.json`; nothing reads them; leave alone (player data).

# Sky Strike (id `sky_raider`) pack v8 (2026-10-07)
Vertical shooter, relative-drag steering, tiny hitbox, P/B/1UP drops, battleship per stage. Islands solid (hitbox inside = crash once per island; never two at same height, max 360 wide so one side is open; turrets ride on them). Lost life resets guns to level 1. Battleships escalate per level (`BOSS_PHASES`: 2-4 phases; fans, rings, aimed streams, spirals, walls with one gap, escorts, laser with blinking sight line; armour 400 + 130 lvl + 6 lvl²). Hard harder all round (`DIFFS`). Music from the Music library (stage n = lively n-1, battleship = techno). Balanced with a dodging bot (old battleships: 0 hits at any stage).
