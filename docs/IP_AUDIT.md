# IP / copyright audit — rules and checklist

Disclaimer: this is a practical engineering checklist, not legal advice. For a commercial launch, have an
Argentine IP lawyer review the final names and store listing.

## Principles
- **Mechanics/rules are generally free to use**; names, logos, characters, artwork, distinctive "look and feel"
  (trade dress) and copied rule text are not.
  - US: 17 U.S.C. §102(b) (no protection for ideas, procedures, methods, systems); 37 C.F.R. §202.1 (names,
    titles, short phrases and game ideas aren't copyrightable — but names can be **trademarks**).
  - Look-and-feel limit: *Tetris Holding v. Xio Interactive*, 863 F. Supp. 2d 394 (D.N.J. 2012) — a clone
    copying Tetris's visual expression infringed even though mechanics are free.
  - *Data East USA v. Epyx*, 862 F.2d 204 (9th Cir. 1988) — elements dictated by the genre (scènes à faire) aren't protected.
  - *Spry Fox v. LOLApps/6waves* (W.D. Wash. 2012) — a near-copy of Triple Town survived dismissal; settled.
  - Argentina: Ley 11.723 (Propiedad Intelectual) and Ley 22.362 (Marcas y Designaciones). Both are federal;
    no Tucumán-specific statute; disputes generally go to federal courts. Argentina is party to the Berne Convention.
- Google Play also removes apps for impersonation / misleading metadata (other apps' names in title or keywords).

## Per-game checklist (all must be ✓)
- [ ] Game name is generic or original (not on the blocklist below, not "X-like" in store text).
- [ ] No logos, characters, mascots, or recognisable art/colour schemes copied from the commercial original.
- [ ] Card, tile, board and piece art drawn by us or properly licensed (Spanish deck, poker deck: original drawings).
- [ ] How-to text written in our own words (never pasted from a rulebook or Wikipedia).
- [ ] Sounds/music: own or CC0/CC-BY (credited); none ripped from the original game.
- [ ] Party/drinking card prompts written by us (Cards Against Humanity content is CC BY-NC-SA → **cannot** be used commercially).
- [ ] Voodoo skin assets (skulls, dolls, **lips**) are original; the lips motif must not resemble a famous band/brand
      lips-and-tongue logo.
- [ ] `CREDITS.json` entries complete.
