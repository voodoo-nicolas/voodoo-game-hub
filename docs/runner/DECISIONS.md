# The Runner: decisions log

The owner's design docs (`GAME_DESIGN_THE_RUNNER.md` §0, `GAME_DESIGN_THE_RUNNER_V2.md` §0) ask for every decision to be logged here.
D-1..D-17 are pack v1 (Act I in the old galaxy). **v2 (2026-11-07) supersedes D-2, D-4 (combat), D-5, D-6 and D-13 where they disagree**; the rest still holds.
Owner answers are marked **(owner)**; everything else is a default chosen while building, open to change.

## Settled with the owner (2026-11-02)
- **D-1 (owner)** Id `the_runner`, title "The Runner" in English and Spanish, category Adventure (next to Turned). Ids are never renamed.
- **D-2 (owner)** Fixed hero: the man in the landing art. No look picker in pack v1.
- Single player only, Pad overlay mostly transparent, Menu button → ship computer with six tabs **(owner, kickoff message)**.

## Defaults chosen while building pack v1
- **D-3** Content lives in `scripts/games/the_runner/the_runner_data.gd` as constants, not JSON under `/data` (design doc §0). Why: the hub's translation tool (`hub.py i18n`) only reads English text from `.gd` files; JSON names would never be translated. Same tables, same "no content in the engine" rule.
- **D-4** Engine: Godot / GDScript (design doc open question 1). Art: the owner's paintings as they are (open question 2). Combat: turn-based (Attack / Evade / Run) (open question 3). Monetization: none (hub rule, open question 4). Phone + PC (open question 5). Scope v1: one system, Cradle Reach (open question 6). Tone: gritty with pulp (open question 7).
- **D-5** One system map, `system_1.webp`: ten worlds placed on the picture's own planets (bright-spot detection, not guessed). The Sun and the black hole in the picture are hazards ("The Maw"). The other orbit pictures (`system_2/3`, `system_rings`, `system_star`, `system_blackhole`) are imported for later systems.
- **D-6** Starting ship is the Moth (design doc: the Runner comes with M03). The Runner = more hold, tank, hull, guns, and the Ghost Core (+3 stealth).
- **D-7** No "stims": contraband is forged IDs, bootleg data, unlicensed weapons, stolen tech, Hollow relics. Keeps the game out of the `drug_reference` rating descriptor. Manifest content: 12+, `violence_cartoon` (owner to confirm).
- **D-8** Scan formula: design doc §9 with base 0.65 (not 0.5) and law weighted 0.04 (not 0.08): the doc's numbers gave an 11% pass chance at Luna with a clean hidden hold. Contraband in the open hold multiplies the pass chance by 0.35.
- **D-9** Pirate tribute = 100 + 15% of cargo value + 5% of your credits (max 2,000). A share of all your money (first version) worked as a wealth tax: the 10,000-trip simulation never got past ~36k.
- **D-10** Safety net: broke, nothing to sell, can't move → Mother Kessel lends 500 (owe 600), any port. A tow when the tank runs dry costs 500 or goes on the debt. No run can soft-lock.
- **D-11** Unpaid fines no longer become debt: they take what you have.
- **D-12** Dirty credits pay for contraband, black-market parts, bribes, tribute, fuel, repairs and the debt. Legal goods and legal parts need clean credits. Net worth = clean + half the parts' price + cargo at base price + 30% dirty − debt.
- **D-13** Skill tree = the six specializations of design doc §7.2, three ranks each, all live. First branch learned = primary, second = secondary, the rest stay locked.
- **D-14** Hints (owner rule 2026-11-01): the Fixer's tip in the Market names the best trade from here. 3 at most, +1 every 24 h, in `user://the_runner_hints.json` (outside the run save, so a new run doesn't refill them).
- **D-15** Leaderboard: "Best net worth" (higher is better; only positive values post). Act I done = `info.result("win")` once.
- **D-16** The Pad shows only on the system map (stick flies, A = GO / DOCK, B = STOP, its pause and menu pills replace the status bar's ⏸ and ☰ there). Dock, market and menus are tap screens: the Pad hides. `_pad()` is not defined (owner 2026-10-29: Controls in Options stay hidden).
- **D-17** Saving: the kit's pause saves the whole run (`user://the_runner_save.json`, version 1), including an open encounter and the ship's place mid-flight.

Pack v1 LIVE 2026-11-02 (4.9 MB; art imported lossy). Not phone-tested.

## v2: the Spiral (owner's design doc v2, applied 2026-11-07)

### Settled with the owner (2026-11-07)
- **D-18 (owner)** Character creation as in v2 §2 (name, gender, race, home faction, 2 perk picks), replacing D-2 (fixed hero). The landing art stays as the title picture; races show as coloured emblems until the owner paints portraits.
- **D-19 (owner)** Pack v1 runs (Moon, Old Earth, the old factions) can't carry over: ▶ Resume on a v1 save says so and opens character creation. 🏆, 🏅 and stats stay (they live outside the run save).
- **D-20 (owner)** The six-branch Skill Tree stays as the XP level-up track beside v2's perks.

### Defaults chosen while building v2
- **D-3 still holds**: the v2 tables are `.gd` constants (`the_runner_data.gd` = world, `the_runner_spiral.gd` = people), not `/data/*.json`, so `hub.py i18n` can translate them. `D.CONFIG` holds v2 §0's flags (`starting_perk_picks` 2, `crew_flaw_strength` 0.5, `perk_stack_cap` 2, `battle_mode` "realtime").
- **D-21** Heat is one bar with the Luminary Accord: every Paragon world scans; lawful independent worlds scan at half your heat; Pact worlds never scan. The doc names no per-faction heat.
- **D-22** World: 19 planets on 3 sector pictures (`system_1` Cradle Reach, `system_2` Veil Expanse, `system_3` Ember Drift), placed by eye on a coordinate grid over each picture's own planets. The Hollow Deep's 3 planets (22 in all) are listed; its gate stays shut (Act III). Each sector has a gate; Veil Expanse and Ember Drift open with Act I (flag `act1_done`, quest N04). A jump: 2 fuel, 1 day.
- **D-23** Flight time (v2 §1.1): autopilot trip = clamp(16 + px/30, 20, 45) s, divided by the speed factor (Pilot, Aerie 50%, extra engines, engine model). The map camera follows the ship at 2.3× the fitted picture; 🔭 shows the whole sector. The ship is drawn 1/6 of the screen wide.
- **D-24** Visible parts (v2 §1.2): the ship is drawn from shared-library pieces (Kenney space set, CC0): hull by tier, wings from Courier up, one turret per weapon socket (sprite by gun model), one engine per engine socket (sprite by engine model) with flames, cargo pods for bigger racks, antennae for scanners, shield bubble (battles), Ghost Core glow, a lit window per crew member. No new art files.
- **D-25** Ships: you start in the Moth (Kessel's rented skiff, 1 crew slot, 1 socket of each kind). Quest N03 gives Old Marrow's Runner as a Skiff (Runner Mk I) with the Ghost Core (+3 stealth, built in, not a part). Shipyards refit the Runner to Courier 25,000 / Corvette 150,000 / Cruiser 1,000,000 (Efficient Refit −10%). One part model per kind goes on every socket of that kind; specials are separate parts, one per special socket (ECM Jammer, Veil Plating, Tractor Beam, Repair Nanites).
- **D-26** Quest steps: the doc gives names, summaries and rewards; what you actually do is filled in (deliver / reach / pay / spend / scans / fights / battle at a world / salvage n times / have n crew). Rewards (credits, faction deltas, bar points) are the doc's numbers exactly; only the factions a quest lists change (§7.4). N02's "3,000 credits" is read as the 3,000 debt you pay. P09 and F02 let you pick the faction; C11, C13, P12 and O10 end with the doc's choices.
- **D-27** Faction quests need 10% rep with the giver's faction (§7.4). Exceptions: main quests, crew discovery quests, neutral quests, independent worlds, and O01 (the Outlaw intro). Fixer contracts give the issuer +50 FP (a dirty job also −50 FP to the destination's Accord holder) and every 3rd Accord / black-market job +1 Paragon / Outlaw (§11.6). Bounty contracts: the Hunt-Guilds' fixers post only bounties; others sometimes.
- **D-28** Rep: −500..1000 FP. 25% = −5% prices, 50% = −10%, below 0 = +20% and no quests, below −25% = their ships attack on arrival and the port is closed except fuel (so no run can strand itself). Bonuses at 50/75/100% are lost when rep drops (doc default, open question 5). Rep repair at the faction's own office: 1,000 credits per +10%, up to 50%.
- **D-29** Bars: Paragon and Outlaw, 0..100, never decrease (doc default, open question 3). Crew of a bloc need 3 on its bar; a Paragon and an Outlaw crew member aboard each lose 15 loyalty (open question 4, kept as written).
- **D-30** Perk engine (§5.3): same perk from two sources = 100% + 50%, no third; per stat, good perks and bonuses cap at +60%, flaws at −40%. Crew quirks count at 50%. Flags (Hacker, Lucky, Intimidating, Tactician, Cartographer, Contract Broker) are on/off. The optional race synergy (+3%, §3.1) is on. The six unused flaws come back as curses: cursed cargo pods and a "Faustian bargain" event (dirty credits for 6 days of a flaw). Haggler can't sell above the dock's own buy price (no buy-and-sell loop).
- **D-31** Real-time battles (§1.3, `the_runner_battle.gd`): one arena; you steer (stick / drag / WASD), tap or press A to change target; guns fire by themselves within range 600; enemies keep ~360 away and circle. Damage per shot 6 × gun model × perks; hit = accuracy × (1 − evasion); moving fast adds 10% evasion. Buttons: Shields (+50%, 14 s), Evade (2.5 s, every 9 s), Flee (3 s jump spool, then the flee roll), Hack (once per battle with Hacker), Board (target under 25% hull, boardable ships), and the once-per-run calls (escort wing, wingmates, raid fleet, allied fleet, spore cloud, Shadow Cloak). Duels never capture you. The encounter card before a battle keeps v2's options: fight, flee, bribe, bluff, surrender cargo, board, dump & run. `battle_mode` is fixed at "realtime"; the kit's pause menu pauses a battle.
- **D-32** Crew wages are paid daily (any credits); unpaid crew lose 8 loyalty a day and leave at 0 (they wait at their planet). Hiring fees 1,500-4,000 (Pip is free and joins when C01 is done if a slot is free).
- **D-33** Achievements: 11 new (crew, bars, hull tiers, rep, quests), keyed on "Highest / Most / Best" stats so the hub's generic badge tiers don't grow; "Contracts done" became "Most contracts done" for the same reason. Total 950 points (cap 1000).
- **D-34** Pad sub-labels now go through `tr("Dock").to_upper()`: the old ALL-CAPS literals (DOCK / GO / STOP) never reached the Spanish pack (the extractor skips them).
