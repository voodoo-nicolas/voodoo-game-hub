## 1. One-liner
1900. You wake up thirsty, hungover without having drunk, with two holes in your neck. Every day the sun hurts more. Feed to stay alive, hide to stay unseen. Some villagers know what you are becoming. You don't.

## 2. Tone and setting
- Gothic, small cities, year 1900. Gas lamps, rail, churches, markets, bridges, rooftops, sewers, cemeteries.
- No smartphones, no cars. Horse carts, telegraph, early electric lights (new and scary).
- Fictional towns (placeholders **[DECIDE]**): **Vellmoor** (start), **Marrowgate**, **Saltmere**, **Dunwick Ferry**, linked by rail, one town per act.
- Look: dark palette, warm lamp pools, cold blue shade. Light = danger and visibility.

## 3. Protagonist
- Both genders playable, same story. Placeholder names: **Damian** (male, **[DECIDE]** final) and a female counterpart **[DECIDE]**.
- Ordinary person at the start. **[DECIDE]** occupation (clockmaker's apprentice, nurse, telegraph clerk, seamstress). Occupation can give one starting skill.
- Voice: confused, dry, scared. Inner lines shown as short text, no voice acting.

## 4. Core story question
What happened last night, who did it, and why do some people in town already seem to know?

## 5. The Knowing (new)
Some villagers know who you are and what you are becoming. You don't. Most villagers don't.
- **How they know [DECIDE]:**
  - **A. The town has seen this before (recommended).** A past turning happened here. A few families kept the secret. They recognize the signs.
  - **B. A secret circle.** A small society watches for the turned. Some want to help, some to hunt.
  - **C. Your own past.** You were known to them before: relative, old friend, someone who owed you. They watched it coming.
- **Roles:**
  - **Friend:** warns you, hides you, hands out clues. Speaks in half-truths.
  - **Watcher:** observes, reports to others. Polite, unsettling.
  - **Hunter:** tracks you. Sets up silver and lamps.
  - **Devotee:** wants to serve or be turned. Feeding on them has a heavy story cost.
- **Tell, don't spell out.** They say things like "You should not be out in that sun", or leave you a cloak and a locked box. Unaware villagers only see pale skin, a flinch, a scarf over the neck.
- Their reactions change as vampirism worsens. Early: sympathy and worry. Later: fear, hatred, or reverence.
- Their lines are the main source of clues and Missions.

## 6. Mystery options [DECIDE]
- **A. Unfinished turning.** The bite was interrupted. You are mid-transformation, and it keeps worsening until finished or reversed. Feeding stalls it. The Knowing recognize an unfinished turning, because it has happened here before.
- **B. Experiment.** Someone tests a "cure for the day". You are the subject.
- **C. Inheritance.** The bite woke something in your bloodline. The sire is a relative or old family friend.

Recommended combo: **Knowing A + Mystery A.** The Knowing remember what happened to the last unfinished one.

## 8. Gradual vampirism = story beats
| Day | Sun effect | Story beat | Knowing villagers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skin burns a little. Tiny HP drain. Too bright. | Denial. You go about your day. | One person stares at your neck and says nothing. |
| 2 | Sun hurts. Squint, blur. Avoid open streets. | Fear. Someone notices you flinching. | A Friend leaves a warning or a cloak. |
| 3 | "No. This is not OK." Sun is dangerous. Shade only. | Acceptance. First feed decision. Claws awaken (button X). | A Watcher follows you. A Hunter makes a move. |
| 4+ | Direct sun near-lethal. | Night life. Rooftops, alleys, sewers. | Alliances and enemies form. |

Feeding gives back a little "day-walking" and restarts timers, with diminishing returns.

## 8b. Vampire rules (owner, 2026-10-23; the traditional ones)
- **Sun burns.** Already built (Health, shade, sun clock).
- **Silver** burns them: touching it hurts, a burn that stays red and swollen. Harmless to everyone else (the pocket watch, Scene 6).
- **Garlic**: they cannot be around it (sickness / repelled).
- **A wooden stake through the chest** kills them.
- **Mirrors**: "supposedly" no reflection. **[DECIDE]** how it fits the built prologue, where the mirror shows the neck. Proposed: the reflection is dim, wrong and fading while the turning is unfinished (it still shows on Day 1, gone by Day 5: the bible's §10 question).
- **Hunger.** Not feeding makes them weak, thin, aggravated and out of mood. Feeding restores health, strength, motivation, feeling alive. (Built so far: Blood does not drain yet; hunger as a mood and body state is new.)
- **Water** hurts the player (Scene 5: it burns, coughing, blackout). Not in the traditional list: owner's rule.
- **Night and day.** The game has a clock: the sun comes and goes and so does the moon, which lights the night (owner: "a light for the moon that comes and goes").

## 9. Feeding and morality
- Criminals / predators: safe, small cost.
- Animals: weak, costs time.
- Strangers (innocents): strong, but witnesses, guilt events, hunters follow.
- Someone who trusts you (or a Devotee): strongest, biggest story cost.

Choices change how villagers react and the ending.

## 10. Opening scene script (prologue, about 60 seconds of play)
1. **Wake.** Black screen. Heartbeat, muffled street. Fade in on a small bedroom, dim morning light. Inner text: *"I'm thirsty."*
2. **Headache.** Prompt: **press A to sit up.** Hands through hair, hold the head. Inner text: *"What happened last night?"* Nothing comes. Body aches. *"Like a hangover. But I didn't drink."*
3. **Face.** Prompt: **press A to wipe your face.** Hand over face, deep sigh, hand slides down the back of the neck, then the side.
4. **The feeling.** Fingers stop. Two small holes. It hurts, and strangely, a little good. *"What is this?"* Muffled pulse swell.
5. **Move.** First control: d-pad. Walk to the bathroom.
6. **Mirror.** Press A at the mirror. Close-up on the neck. Two marks. *"What is going on?"*
7. **End.** Leave the room. Objective appears in Missions: *"Find out what happened."*

Small details: first clues in the room (torn invitation, a stranger's coat, a note with half a name). A cloak or scarf hanging by the door. **[DECIDE]** whether the mirror reflection fades by day 5.
