## LOCKED: Core premise

* Game title: **Turned**.
* Opening (Part 1): player wakes up with neck bites, wearing a stranger's coat. The watch is found in the coat.
* Ending: **the player chooses between the cure and immortality.** Vamp B embodies both options.
* Each generation of vampire is weaker: A is the original, B is a weaker version, the player is turned from B's blood.
* The old man's recipe is the original and controls the thirst. The harbor man's remedy is a weaker version of it.

## LOCKED: The chain of events behind the story

1. **Vamp A** made **Vamp B**.
2. **Vamp B** was a member of an organization (see below). B pursued theories and experiments that contradicted the organization's purpose, went where he did not belong, and vanished roughly 50 to 60 years ago.
3. **The Old Man** had a personal relationship with B. He experimented on B. He feels guilt and regrets what he did.
4. **The church** discovered the old man's connection to B and **exiled him** for experiments and blasphemy.
5. **B turned the player because the player is a blood relative of A.** Not random: B was drawn to A's bloodline.
6. The player, the old man, and B all know each other but **none of them realizes the connection until the end**, when the player is finally found and faces the cure-or-immortality choice.

## LOCKED: The organization and the watch

* A signa (sigil) and initials are engraved on the watch found in the coat.
* The old man recognizes the sigil. He believed the organization was long dead and more myth than fact.
* The harbor man is a living member, proving the organization survives quietly.
* The harbor man recognizes the initials: they belong to B, the member who vanished 50 to 60 years ago.
* The harbor man says everything he knows about the watch is mostly rumors.

## Part 2 storyline (written beats)

1. **Old man's house.** Player and shopkeeper reach his forest house. He recognizes the symptoms and has seen the change before.
2. **The recipe.** He knows a recipe that can control the thirst. It needs a specific list of herbs from the harbor.
3. **The watch.** The shopkeeper mentions it. The old man recognizes the sigil and says the organization was thought a myth.
4. **Temptation.** The player keeps complaining of hunger and can smell and hear the shopkeeper's pulse and blood. The shopkeeper is bleeding and the player gets irritable from thirst. The old man tells the shopkeeper to leave, and he does.
5. **Next morning.** The old man cannot accompany the player to the harbor and does not explain why (the reason is his exile). He tells the player where to go and who to talk to.
6. **The sun.** At first tolerable, then unbearable. The player must find shade.

   * Steals an unattended hat to keep going.
   * Finds shade wherever possible and a scarf to wrap around the face.
7. **The harbor.** Player finds the dock and ship and asks for the man mentioned. Locals stare at the clothes. The player is weak, struggling, slightly confused. They let the player sit in the captain's quarters and offer water.
8. **The wait.** The thirst becomes painful. The harbor man arrives, takes one look, and understands. The player hands over the herb list, then blacks out.
9. **Waking.** The player comes to and notices the harbor man has the same watch.
10. **The calming dose.** The harbor man gave the player something that finally calms the thirst. Strength returns slowly.
11. **The watch revealed.** The player asks about the watch. "Rumors mostly." Then he looks at the player's watch and his attention sharpens. The initials are familiar for one reason: the member who vanished decades ago.
12. **The implication.** The vanished member went looking and seemingly found what he searched for. Perhaps he was turned all those years ago, and now he has turned the player, for whatever unlucky reason (the real reason is the bloodline, hidden from everyone).
