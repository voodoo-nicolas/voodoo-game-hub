## 2. Data we collect

- **Account:** email address, username, avatar/profile and language. Your password is handled by the authentication system and we never see it in plain text.
- **Age:** only a bracket (13–15, 16–17, 18 or over). We do not store your exact date of birth.
- **Gameplay:** scores, leaderboards, achievements, 1–5 star ratings, play time and per-game statistics.
- **Social:** friends list and friend requests.
- **Access and purchases:** status of free trials, individual purchases and subscription, and Google Play purchase identifiers/tokens. We do not receive or store your card details.
- **Technical data:** device model, Android version, app version and language.
- **Consents:** which version of these documents you accepted and when.
- **Camera:** the drawing minigame uses the camera only on your device to show the image to trace. Images are not sent to or stored on our servers.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, microphone or payment card data.

## 3. How we use data

- Create and maintain your account and sign you in.
- Show leaderboards, achievements, your profile and friends.
- Manage trials, purchases and subscription.
- Show only the games suitable for your age bracket.
- Improve games using aggregated statistics (players, play time, ratings).
- Keep the service secure, prevent cheating and enforce the Terms.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Consent

We process your data with your free, express and informed consent (art. 5, Argentine Law 25,326), given when you create your account. You can withdraw it by deleting your account.

## 5. Who we share data with

We use providers that process data on our behalf:

- **Supabase** (database and authentication). Hosting region: {{REGION_SUPABASE}}.
- **Google (Google Play and Google Play Billing):** app distribution and payment processing.
- **GitHub** (content and game update delivery).

We do not sell your data. The Hub does not show third-party ads or build advertising profiles. We may disclose data if a competent judicial or administrative authority requires it.

## 6. International transfers

Our providers may store data outside Argentina. We make these transfers with appropriate contractual safeguards and with your consent (art. 12, Law 25,326; DNPDP Disposition 60-E/2016). Our database provider (Supabase) includes in its terms a data processing agreement with standard contractual clauses.

## 7. Retention

While your account is active. ## 8. Your rights

You can access, rectify, update and delete your data (arts. 14, 16 and related, Law 25,326). Access is free at intervals of at least six months unless a legitimate interest is shown (art. 14, para. 3).

- **Delete your account:** Settings → Account → Delete account, or at {{URL_ELIMINAR_CUENTA}}.
- **Other requests:** write to {{EMAIL_PRIVACIDAD}}. We answer within legal deadlines (10 calendar days for access; 5 business days for rectification or deletion, per Decree 1558/2001).

The Argentine Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP), as the supervisory authority of Law 25,326, handles complaints from people whose rights are affected by non-compliance with personal data protection rules.

## 9. Minors

Between {{EDAD_MINIMA}} and 17, use requires the knowledge and authorization of a parent or guardian, and purchases require an adult responsible for the payment method. Parents and guardians can request account deletion at {{EMAIL_PRIVACIDAD}}.
