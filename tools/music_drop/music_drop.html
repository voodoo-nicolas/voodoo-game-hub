Viral Music Drop

Add music to the hub's shared library. Converted on this PC, then sent to GitHub. Phones download a track when a game needs it.

Pace is how the game uses it: Slow (calm, under ~95 BPM) for menus and puzzles, Moderate (lively, ~95-130) for arcade levels, Fast (intense, over ~130) for bosses and finales.

Remove takes a track off the list, and phones stop playing it. Its file stays on the GitHub release.

Games that play music ask for a pace (calm, lively or intense) and get a track of that pace. Here you choose which genres each category or game uses (none picked = any genre), and give a game background music by picking a style for it. Off = no music in that game. A game's own setting wins over its category's.
